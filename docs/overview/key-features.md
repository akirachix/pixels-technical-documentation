# Key Features

MvuviGo's key features focus on enabling small-scale fishers on Lake Victoria to raise emergency alerts, share relevant location information, identify nearby responders, and coordinate assistance. 

## 1. Raise an alarm without a smartphone or mobile coverage

| Capability | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **SOS button** | One press starts an emergency | Raising an alarm must be simple under stress |
| **GPS location capture** | Records the latest available location with the alert | Rescuers need to know where the boat is |
| **Long-range radio transmission** | Sends the alert to the landing station and nearby devices | The first alert does not depend on mobile coverage |
| **Buzzer and display** | Gives local sound and visual feedback | Shows the fisher the device is working |

## 2. Reach the people closest to the incident

| Capability | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Emergency notification** | Alerts the landing station and nearby participating fishers together | Nearby fishers are often the fastest help |
| **Responder location sharing** | Nearby devices share their positions | Shows who is close enough to help |

## 3. Guide and coordinate the response

| Capability | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Emergency dashboard** | Shows the emergency, boat locations and distances | Gives the operator one view of the incident |
| **Automated directions** | Creates directions from each responder to the distressed boat | Helps responders find the boat, especially at night |
| **Feature-phone messaging** | Sends directions as messages | Responders do not need smartphones |
| **30-second updates** | Regenerates directions from the latest positions | Boats move, so old directions mislead |
| **Operator coordination** | The operator contacts responders by phone | Keeps rescue human-led |

## 4. Run and learn from the service

| Capability | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Fisher registration and device pairing** | Links each fisher to a unique device | Alerts are tied to the right person |
| **RFID identification** | Identifies the registered fisher during use | Confirms who is in distress |
| **Incident recording** | Logs the incident, responses, status and outcome | Supports follow-up and safety planning |
