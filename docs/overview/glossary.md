# Glossary

## Roles

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Fisher in distress** | The fisher who presses SOS |
| **Responding fisher (responder)** | A nearby participating fisher who receives an alert and may go to help |
| **Participating fisher** | A fisher registered with MvuviGo and carrying a paired device |
| **Landing-site operator** | The person at the landing site who registers fishers and devices, monitors the dashboard, coordinates the response and records the incident. Always called "operator" in this documentation |
| **Small-scale fisher** | A person who fishes for a living from a small boat near a landing site |

## Places and organisations

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Landing site** | A shore location where boats launch and land their catch |
| **Landing station** | The radio receiving equipment and computer at a landing site |
| **Beach Management Unit (BMU)** | A community body at a landing site that takes part in managing fishing activity |
| **Maritime rescue coordination centre** | A government centre that coordinates search and rescue |

## System terms

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **SOS** | An emergency alert raised by pressing the button on a MvuviGo device |
| **MVP** | Minimum viable product: the first version, meant to test the full alert-to-record workflow |
| **Latest available location** | The most recent GPS position the device has, which may not be current. The system must distinguish it from a current position |
| **Decision support** | Guidance offered to people, who make the final decisions |
| **Feature phone** | A basic mobile phone that makes calls and sends messages but does not run apps |

## Technology

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **LoRa** | A long-range, low-power radio technology that works without mobile coverage |
| **GPS** | Global Positioning System, used to find a location |
| **RFID** | Radio-frequency identification, used here to identify the fisher linked to a device |
| **OLED** | A small display on the device |
