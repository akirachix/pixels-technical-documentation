# MvuviGo

**MvuviGo is an emergency-response system for small-scale fishers on Lake Victoria.** A fisher in danger presses one button on a small device. The device sends an SOS with the fisher's identity and latest GPS location over long-range radio, without needing mobile coverage, to the landing site and to nearby participating fishers. A landing-site operator coordinates the rescue, nearby responders receive directions to the distressed boat, and the incident is recorded.

## Primary purpose

To shorten the gap between **a boat getting into trouble** and **someone nearby knowing where it is and acting**.

## What it is for, and for whom

| If you are a... | MvuviGo lets you... |
|---|---|
| Fisher in distress | Press one button to send an SOS with your latest available location |
| Nearby fisher | Be alerted, share your position and receive directions toward the distressed boat |
| Landing-site operator | See the emergency, nearby boats and distances; coordinate by phone; record the incident |

## What it does not do

MvuviGo supports **human-led** rescue. It does not forecast weather, steer boats, protect a person in the water, replace formal search and rescue, or provide its own calling service. See [System Boundaries](overview/scope-and-status.md#system-boundaries).
