# Communication Systems

Welcome to the Communication Systems documentation for MvuviGo.

Operating on Lake Victoria presents a massive challenge: standard cellular networks are often unreliable or non-existent in deep waters. Relying on mobile data for maritime safety is not an option for small-scale fishers.

To address this, MvuviGo uses a combination of long-range radio, RFID, and SMS technology. This section explains how the hardware and communication layers work together to bridge the gap between the open water and the landing site, ensuring that no distress signal is lost.
