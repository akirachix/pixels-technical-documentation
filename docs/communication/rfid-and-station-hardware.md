# RFID and Station Hardware

The landing site is the practical unit of community life around the lake. In MvuviGo, the landing site hosts a physical station and an operator who coordinates emergency responses.

## Hardware Components
The system uses a landing-site station equipped with:
* **LoRa Communication:** To receive SOS alerts and location data from devices on the water.
* **A Dashboard:** A screen where the operator can see active emergencies, the distressed boat, and participating boats.

## Registration and RFID
Before a fisher goes out on the water, they must be registered by the landing-site operator.
* **Device Pairing:** The operator registers participating fishers and pairs them with a unique MvuviGo device.
* **RFID Identification:** Radio-frequency identification (RFID) is used to identify the fisher associated with a specific device.

## The Operator's Role
When an SOS is triggered, the landing-site operator is a critical human link in the system. 
* They monitor the dashboard to view the emergency.
* They coordinate the response by normal phone call.
* They record the incident, including responses, locations, status, and outcome.
